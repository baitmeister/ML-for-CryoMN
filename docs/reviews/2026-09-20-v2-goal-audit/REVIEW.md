**1. Overall confidence: 40%.**

This is my subjective confidence that the current computational implementation can reliably guide discovery of a formulation meeting the stated application goal. It is not a statistical probability of campaign success, a calibrated model confidence, or an average of test pass rates.

The limiting chain is: formulation → measured compression endpoint → resistance to failure during application → stratum-corneum penetration → viable delivered cells. The code optimizes formulation-dependent viability and a compression endpoint. The remaining links are not established in the reviewed evidence. Prediction errors, limited mechanical evidence, and reproducible data-lifecycle defects further reduce confidence. Successful code execution alone cannot close these gaps.

**VERIFIED–execution:** 411 formulation records, 965 observation records, 24 active ingredient features, 10 measured campaign rounds, 420 campaign viability readouts, and no duplicate observation IDs. There are 13 mechanical attempts with stored terminal analyses: 12 complete traces and one incomplete trace. These yield only **8 comparable formulation–batch objective pairs across 8 formulations and 2 batches**, not 12 independent formulations. All 13 analyses reproduce from their original raw files, including hashes, statuses and terminal forces. The active Round 11 phase is `mechanics_hybrid`.

I therefore treat 0.17 N/needle as the user's soft target, not a universal skin-insertion threshold. [Davis et al., 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15212920/). Its mechanical value comes from one valid specimen. This is **not verified application success**, not an unbiased success-rate estimate, and not evidence of cross-batch reliability.

The new model must be judged separately from the older production workflow. Frozen pre-outcome Round 10 challenger evidence gives the batch/control strategies MAE **18.33 points**, coverage **81.8%**, and width **68.38 points** over 11 ordinary formulations. All ordinary predictions overestimate that batch. The campaign-only fitted-noise alternative has MAE **17.80 points**. The reported **5.48-point** control-strategy MAE occurs **after revealing designated same-batch reference/retest results**, on eight remaining formulations; it is not pre-experiment selection accuracy. **3. Important assumptions.**

| Assumption | Implementation/evidence status |
|---|---|
| The 24 ingredient concentrations adequately characterize response. | Verified feature set. Geometry, temperature, thaw delay, cooling/storage protocol, cell passage/donor and assay conditions are not GP input features. Treating these as sufficiently constant is an assumption. |
| One campaign group represents one biological batch. | Explicit in frozen GP manifest; actual cell-batch/preparation IDs are absent from viability records. |
| Batch effects shift viability additively and similarly across recipes and the control. | Implemented by shared batch covariance and a separate uncertain control intercept. Recipe-by-batch interactions and heteroscedastic batch effects are not represented. |
| A common control response with prior SD 50 percentage points is appropriate. | Recorded decision; sensitivity at 25/50/100 points was checked. This does not validate common response across batches. |
| Formulation–batch means are the relevant optimization units. | Implemented. Replicate means are used without claiming unverified independence-based noise reduction. Reliability of individual patches is a different estimand. |
| Technical replicates can be summarized by a mean; intact means all measured replicates pass. | Implemented. The all-pass outcome depends on how many replicates were measured; differing replicate counts change its interpretation. |
| Viability and mechanical latent objectives are independent. | Implemented by independent-output acquisition models. Correlated biological/physical trade-offs are learned only indirectly through the selected locations, not a cross-output covariance. |
| Fixed Matérn distances after standardization are meaningful in this sparse chemistry space. | Implemented; not validated by predictive superiority on the current mechanical sample. |
| Whole-patch force divided by loaded count is a useful nominal per-needle proxy. | Implemented. It is not a lower bound on each needle's strength and assumes enough loading comparability for ranking. |
| Registry ceilings and aggregate burden rules sufficiently screen preparation risks. | Implemented. Many limits are practical heuristics or room-temperature aqueous-solubility inferences; mixture behavior, freeze concentration, viscosity and pH still require measurement. |
| Count-based phase thresholds establish enough information to use mechanics acquisition. | Implemented thresholds are 8/6/2 and 16/12/3. They do not test predictive accuracy, calibration, diversity, or proximity to the application's target region. |
| Files are advanced by a single operator/process only after results are ready. | Necessary operational assumption; not enforced reliably by the current lifecycle. |

My review assumes the stated 0.17 N and >50% are soft targets, not authorized hard exclusion rules. I have not assumed that “viability” means retained function or viable cell yield after actual insertion. I also have not assumed the database's blank provenance fields mean the laboratory lacks those records elsewhere.

**5. Investigations and validation evidence not located.**

- A prospective relationship between the current terminal-force endpoint and successful stratum-corneum penetration for the actual geometry, array and application conditions.
- Separation of initial stiffness, first irreversible damage, terminal compression, backing contact and actual penetration; and sensitivity to temperature, compression speed and delay outside storage. The SOP itself estimates about 64 seconds from trigger to endpoint and says the CSV cannot establish specimen temperature or absence of melting.
- Repeated independent preparation and biological batches for the numerical proxy hit, including lower-tail mechanical performance and uncertainty around viability >50%.
- Viable delivered-cell yield, recovery/function after release, and viability at the intended time after actual application. A viability percentage without its denominator, assay time and delivery yield cannot establish the whole application outcome.
- Randomized/block-balanced evidence supporting an additive common batch shift and validating the control across formulations, operators and cell batches.
- Held-out prospective improvement in discovering target-region formulations over feasible random search, a small designed experiment, or simple local search. Retrospective prediction comparison and frozen challenger scoring on the incumbent's slate are not counterfactual optimizer trials.
- Ablation/sensitivity across pool seed and size, search bounds, diversity caps, similarity thresholds, intact priors, phase gates and acquisition choices. Prior-SD sensitivity alone does not cover these.
- Empirical calibration of exact-combination intact probabilities and of future-measurement uncertainty. Three formulations per ingredient is not validation of ingredient interactions.
- Measured mixture feasibility under preparation and freezing conditions, including solubility, homogeneity, viscosity/fillability, osmolality/pH and material-grade consistency. There are no preparation-feasibility endpoint observations in the current database, so its learned preparation model is inactive.

**7. YAGNI / complexity pass.**

The 63 v2 Python files contain roughly **19,278 lines**, of which helpers account for 17,710. Line count alone is not a defect. The concern is interacting policy code that is harder to validate than the amount of mechanical evidence currently supports.

**VERIFIED–source:** `select_next_round` selects and validates a slate, then `apply_group10` refits models, reconstructs evidence, rebuilds a second slate, reimplements several constraints and reassigns mechanical order. This makes earlier metadata and policy guarantees easy to invalidate. The second layer hardcodes values such as 12 screens, four mechanical positions and cold-start counts alongside separately validated configuration. A future config edit may affect the first layer but not the final slate. [Second selection pass](</Users/doggonebastard/Antigravity/ML for CryoMN/src/08_multi_objective/helper/group10_selection.py:47>).

The active finite-pool acquisition evaluates candidates individually with `q=1`; subsequent batch selection uses ranking and composition-diversity heuristics. It is not joint optimization of the final four-experiment batch or sequential conditioning on those chosen experiments. This can be a pragmatic design, but should be evaluated against simpler selection and described precisely. Continuous sparse-mask machinery remains available for other strategies; it is not used by the recorded Round 11 strategy. [Shared scores](</Users/doggonebastard/Antigravity/ML for CryoMN/src/08_multi_objective/helper/gp_strategy.py:82>).

Do not add a more elaborate optimizer before endpoint relevance, noise, future-batch calibration and the existing policy stack are tested. Do not remove historical replay, provenance, diagnostic models or existing candidate functionality simply because the active strategy does not use every branch.

**8. Unnecessary abstractions, helpers or code candidates.**

- Replace the second full slate-construction pass with reserved roles supplied to a single constraint/selection pipeline. Preserve all current roles, policy versions and historical behavior; validate invariants after the final allocation.
- The compatibility wrappers in `selection.py` temporarily mutate globals in other modules so tests can patch old names. They introduce process-global state and an additional thread-race risk. Preserve public entry points with simple delegation and explicit test seams. [Global bridges](</Users/doggonebastard/Antigravity/ML for CryoMN/src/08_multi_objective/helper/selection.py:105>).
- Several split modules retain dozens of imports never referenced internally. An AST pass found 68 in `selection_reporting.py`, 52 in `selection_policy.py`, 44 in `selection_constraints.py`, and 36 in `selection_scoring.py`. Some may be deliberate compatibility exports; audit callers before removal. These counts are candidates, not proof of dead functionality.
- Configuration dictionaries carry `_gp_raw_observations`, a DataFrame, and mix runtime state with serializable policy. Pass observations and fitted model objects explicitly so frozen policy metadata has one clear meaning.
- Required challenger fitting/rendering is embedded in the production proposal path. Failure of an unrelated challenger can prevent an otherwise supported selected strategy from publishing. Preserve challenger artifacts, but define which failures truly invalidate production selection and separate rendering from the commit boundary.
- Preserve existing diagnostic stiffness and intact classifier outputs, but correct their documented roles. A diagnostic output should not be presented as an active decision mechanism. Likewise keep historical endpoint compatibility without suggesting old field names are current physical definitions.
- Avoid a new plugin, framework or generalized policy DSL to solve these issues. A single final invariant check, explicit state transition and consolidated data/model contract address the concrete problems.
