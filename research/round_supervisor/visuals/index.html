Data, methods and source files

The evaluation covers R13–R19. The historical slates contain 12 formulations per round, including a fixed reference. Every nonreference formulation has a measured viability. The historical lab selected a subset for compression. A paired case requires an intact patch and both endpoint means in one formulation and round.

The frozen model is a Gaussian process (GP). The three-pick comparison enumerates all 165 triplets on each tested 11-candidate slate. The similar-recipe comparator uses the most recent earlier exact recipe when available. Otherwise it averages the historical means of the three closest distinct recipes in registry-scaled ingredient coordinates. The earlier-round comparator averages earlier nonreference formulation–round means. Both comparators may use observations preceding R13.

The mechanical endpoint is whole-patch force at 0.8 mm after a sustained 1 N trigger, divided by the number of loaded needles. A point on the measured Pareto frontier has no other observed point at least as high on both endpoints and higher on one. Force evidence is restricted to the tested subset.

The round supervisor was designed retrospectively. The replay reconstructs decisions in time order and reveals archived outcomes only after each draft. A missed or absent measurement remains unknown. R20 figures show a pending new-project draft and conditional branches. Independent preparations and cell-batch IDs are needed to evaluate repeatability.
